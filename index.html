About the Salon

LOCALLY RUN, DETAIL OBSESSED

Kincumber K&N Nails & Spa is a local nail studio in Kincumber Village on the Central Coast, offering acrylics, gel extensions, dip powder, manicures, pedicures and hand‑painted nail art. Whether you're after a classic clean set or something more detailed, our technicians take the time to get it right — walk‑ins are welcome and bookings are recommended for longer services.

Clean lines. Careful hands. Nails that last.
