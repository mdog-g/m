About us

Rooted in traditional technique.

Ettalong Thai & Remedial Massage brings authentic, time-honoured Thai massage techniques to the local Ettalong Beach community. Our therapists work with focused pressure, stretching and rhythm to relieve stress, improve circulation and ease chronic tension — the way it's been practised for generations.

Whether you're after a firm remedial session to work through a specific problem area, or a slower traditional Thai treatment to unwind, we tailor every visit to what your body needs. Walk-ins are always welcome, and we're proud to be part of the Ettalong community.
