Treatments

Every session is adapted on the day to what your body needs — not a fixed routine.

Remedial Massage

Targeted, therapeutic work for chronic tension, injury and pain, assessed and treated on a case-by-case basis.

Relaxation Massage

Slower, flowing strokes that ease stress and switch your nervous system into rest mode.

Deep Tissue Massage

Firm, focused pressure reaching the deeper muscle layers for long-standing tightness.

Pregnancy Massage

Gentle, supportive positioning and pressure designed around the changing needs of each trimester.

Hot Stone Massage

Warmed basalt stones paired with massage strokes to melt away deep, stubborn tension.

Reflexology

Pressure-point work through the feet, used to support balance through the whole body.

Frequently Asked Questions

Arrive a few minutes early so we can talk through what's going on for you, any injuries or areas of concern, and what you'd like to get out of the session. From there your therapist tailors the treatment to suit.

Undress to your own comfort level. You'll always be professionally draped with towels throughout, with only the area being worked on uncovered.

No referral is needed — just get in touch to book a time that suits you.

Many private health funds offer rebates on remedial massage. Bring your health fund card to your appointment and ask us to confirm what your fund covers.

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule, so the time can be offered to someone else.
