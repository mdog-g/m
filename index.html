The Studio

We design rooms that feel considered rather than decorated — quiet, tactile, unmistakably lived-in.

August & Ash is a boutique interior design studio based in Sydney, working on full renovations, new builds and styling projects across the coast and the countryside beyond it. Every project starts from how a room is actually used, then works backwards to how it should feel.

What We Offer

Services

I.

Full-Service Design

Concept through to install — space planning, furniture, lighting, joinery and styling delivered as one continuous project.

II.

Styling & Staging

Furniture curation, art and finishing touches for a move-in-ready home, or a home about to go to market.

III.

Renovation Design

Design direction and documentation for renovations and new builds, working alongside your architect and builder.

IV.

Colour & Materials

Bespoke palettes, finishes and material selections for a single room or a whole house.

How We Work

Process

Discovery

Principal Designer

Jordan Ashworth

Jordan founded August & Ash after a decade designing residential and hospitality interiors across Sydney. The studio's work favours warm materials, considered lighting and rooms built to be lived in — not just photographed.
